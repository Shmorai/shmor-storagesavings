Student storage: keep your things safe between terms

Heading home for the summer or studying abroad? Student storage means you don’t have to ship everything home and back again.

Home or business

Advice for personal storage, business storage and everything in between.

Your options

  • Self-storage unit: rent the smallest unit or locker that fits, near campus or near home.
  • Collection services: some companies collect boxes from your room and return them at the start of term. You pay per box or item.
  • Share a unit: splitting a unit with friends can cut the cost each, but agree in writing who pays what and who holds access.

Tips for students

  • Book early: demand peaks at the end of the academic year
  • Ask about student offers, and check the price after any offer ends
  • Use sturdy, same-sized boxes and label each one
  • Don’t store food, and make sure everything is clean and dry
  • Check your insurance: student contents policies don’t always cover storage

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Student storage: FAQs

What size storage unit does a student need?
A small locker or a unit of around 25 sq ft (about 2.5 m², or 5 × 5 ft) usually fits boxes, a bike and a few small items from a student room.
Can I share a storage unit with friends?
Often yes, but the contract is usually in one person’s name, and they’re responsible for payment and access. Agree how you’ll split costs before you start.
