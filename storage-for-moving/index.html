When storage helps during a move

  • A gap between leaving your old home and getting the keys to the new one
  • Renovating or decorating before you move everything in
  • Moving abroad or into temporary accommodation
  • Downsizing and not yet ready to sell or give away furniture
  • Staging a home for sale by clearing clutter

Your three main options

  • Self-storage unit: you (or your movers) take everything to a facility. Flexible and easy to visit.
  • Portable container storage: a container is dropped at your home, you load it, and it’s stored at a depot or kept on your driveway.
  • Removals company storage: many moving companies store your belongings in their warehouse between collection and delivery. Convenient, but you usually can’t access items until they’re delivered.

Plan the move in and out

  1. Book storage as soon as you know your dates, since facilities can be busy at peak moving times
  2. Pick a size using our size guide
  3. Pack the things you’ll need first at the front of the unit
  4. Keep a list of what’s in each box
  5. Check the notice period, so you aren’t charged for an extra month after you move out

Insurance during a move

Check what’s covered at each stage: on the removal truck, in storage and in transit again. Your mover’s cover, the storage provider’s cover and your own home insurance may each cover different parts, with different limits.

Moving storage: frequently asked questions

How much notice do I need to give a storage facility?
It varies. Many rolling contracts ask for anything from a few days to a month’s notice. Check before you sign so you can time it with your move.
Can movers take my things straight to a storage unit?
Yes. Most removals companies will deliver to a storage facility. Make sure the unit is booked, the facility is open and your movers know the access arrangements.
Is it better to use a container or a storage unit when moving?
A container saves you a trip, as you load it at home. A storage unit is easier to visit during the rental. Compare total costs, including delivery and collection.
