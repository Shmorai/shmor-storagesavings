Container storage vs a storage unit: which is better?

Both keep your belongings safe, but they work very differently. Here’s how to decide.

By the StorageSavings editorial team · 13 October 2026 · 1 min read

Photo: frank mckenna on Unsplash

On this page

  1. Quick comparison
  2. Choose a container if…
  3. Choose a storage unit if…
  4. Compare the total cost

Quick comparison

Portable containerStorage unit
LoadingAt your home, at your own paceYou transport everything to the facility
Access during rentalEasy if on site; limited if at a depotWhenever the facility is open to you
Moving to a new homeSome providers deliver the container to your new addressYou move everything again yourself or with movers
Space neededRoom for the container, and possibly a permitNone at home
Main extra costsDelivery, collection and relocation feesVan hire or movers, plus fuel

Choose a container if…

  • You want to pack at home without hiring a van
  • You’re moving and want one load instead of two
  • You’re renovating and want storage on site

Choose a storage unit if…

  • You’ll need to visit regularly
  • You have nowhere to put a container
  • You want climate control or specific security features
  • You need storage for a long or open-ended period

Prices depend heavily on where you live and how long you need storage. Get quotes for both options and add every fee, including delivery, collection, insurance and van hire. See our guides to container storage and saving on storage.
